Product roadmap

The right workspace for every role

Organizations will manage rosters, staff roles, attendance, events, and offers through a full web workspace. Members will have the convenience of native Android and iOS apps for their clubs, notifications, and digital passes. The service is planned for Google Cloud Platform.

The mobile apps will be built after the core workflows are validated. This demo does not yet include store-ready mobile applications or a GCP production deployment.
