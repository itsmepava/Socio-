Attendance demo

Check in either way

Members can present a short-lived pass for staff to scan, or scan the organization’s rotating code from a signed-in Socio account. Successful check-ins appear in the organization’s attendance log.

{% if g.user and g.user.role == 'organization_admin' and has_admin_permission('members') and g.user.organization.status == 'Approved' %} Open {{ g.user.organization.name }} check-in desk → {% elif member and memberships %} Open your first member pass → {% else %} Sign in to try your real QR flow → {% endif %}
01
Member presents pass

Club staff scans it, checks member and tier details, then confirms attendance.

02
Member scans club code

Member signs in, scans the club QR with the phone camera, and confirms their own check-in.

03
Attendance is recorded

The organization sees the check-in time and method in its attendance log.

The phone screen is a clickable concept. The actual one-time QR flows run in this local web demo. Native Android and iOS apps remain planned work.
